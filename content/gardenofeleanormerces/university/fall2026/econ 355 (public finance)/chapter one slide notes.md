## making the deal 

- **before 1867**
	- crown administers several colonies across what we now know as canada, north of the united states
		- atlantic: NS, NB, PEI, Newfoundland
		- Province of Canada; today, southern ontario and quebec
		- BC on the pacific coast
			- **between 1864, negotiated a federal union**
				- composed of province of canada, NS, and NB joined: the rest followed
	- ==we call it 'the deal'==
- bargain struck in 1867:
	- ==colonies hand over theirmain revenue source; ottawa assumes debt and national responsibilities: provinces keep health care, education, and social services, with direct taxation only==
